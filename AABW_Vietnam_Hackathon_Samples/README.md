# AABW Vietnam Hackathon Samples

Sample projects built for the [Agentic AI Build Week (AABW)](https://aabw.genaifund.ai/) hackathon — July 8-12, 2026, Ho Chi Minh City.

Each project demonstrates a real-world use case powered by the [TinyFish SDK](https://agent.tinyfish.ai/api-keys), mapped to one or more official AABW tracks.

## Projects

| Project | Description | Track(s) | Live Demo |
|---------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| [fareguard](./fareguard) | Corporate travel cost control — live fare tracking across Vietnam airlines and OTAs | Track 02 — Mobility (Tasco), Track 07 — Aviation (Galaxy Holdings / Vietjet) | [fareguard.vercel.app](https://fareguard.vercel.app/) |
| [finsight](./finsight) | Vietnamese B2B market intelligence — leasing, competitor pricing, SME loans, SBV regulatory briefs | Track 10 — Financial Services I (Shinhan), Track 11 — Financial Services II (GoTymeX) | [finsight.vercel.app](https://finsight.vercel.app/) |
| [founder-mode](./founder-mode) | Accelerator and grant application copilot for Vietnam startups | Track 12 — Founder Mode (GenAI Fund) | [founder-mode-brown.vercel.app](https://founder-mode-brown.vercel.app/) |
| [market-pulse](./market-pulse) | Retail intelligence for Vietnam electronics — competitor price tracking and restock alerts | Track 06 — Retail (Phong Vu), Track 08 — Retail & Hospitality (Guardian / The Anam) | [marketpulse-gray.vercel.app](https://marketpulse-gray.vercel.app/) |
| [monai](./monai) | Vietnam F&B trend intelligence — menu gaps, forecasts, regional compare, supplier discovery | Track 04 — F&B (KFC) | [mon-ai-alpha.vercel.app](https://mon-ai-alpha.vercel.app/) |
| [rateradar](./rateradar) | Vietnam bank interest rate comparison — daily sweeps across 5 major banks | Track 10 — Financial Services I (Shinhan), Track 11 — Financial Services II (GoTymeX) | [rateradar-teal.vercel.app](https://rateradar-teal.vercel.app/) |
